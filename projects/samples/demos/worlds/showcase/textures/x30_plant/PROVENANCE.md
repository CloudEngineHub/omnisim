# Provenance — `showcase/textures/x30_plant/`

Every image in this directory is **OmniLink's own work**, drawn by a script
with Pillow for [`x30_plant_inspection.omniworld`](../../x30_plant_inspection.omniworld):
equipment signs (`sign_*.png`), two dial faces (`gauge_p101.png`,
`gauge_level.png`) and a hazard stripe (`hazard.png`). The lettering is
Pillow's bundled default font (`ImageFont.load_default`). No photographs,
third-party artwork, logos or trademarks are present; the tag numbers (T-1,
P-101, E-3, V-7) are invented for this fictional site.
