render: add photo mode and improve reflections, glass and lighting

capture path. Trace owned scene geometry and material textures, importance
sample emissive surfaces and the environment, adapt sampling per pixel, and
optionally denoise with Open Image Denoise. Solid glass supports Fresnel
reflection, Snell refraction, internal reflection and thickness absorption.

Improve live rendering with local reflection captures, complete GGX mip chains,
shadowed moving point/spot lights and a reusable rectangular area light. Correct
roughness-map overrides and include the optional realism house, fixed camera
comparisons, runtime packaging guidance and regression coverage.

checks passed. Native checks cover sampling error, dielectric transport, energy,
denoising, cancellation and reflection mips. Engine checks cover photo output,
area emission, moving-light shadows and robot-camera roughness maps. Final
